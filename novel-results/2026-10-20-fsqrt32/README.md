# IEEE `sqrt.rn.f32` on AGX: a 5-op sequence is correctly rounded on every input in [1, 4) (exhaustive, hardware), ≈ 1.4× Mesa's inexact sqrt

Status: one device (driver-reported M2 Max G14C B1), direct DRM, simulator-gated, 0 faults, hang budget unused.
This is the third of the IEEE fp32 lowerings, after [fma](../2026-10-20-fp32-ieee/README.md) and
[div](../2026-10-20-fdiv32/README.md). CUDA's `sqrtf` compiles to `sqrt.rn.f32` unless fast math is enabled.
Mesa lowers `sqrt(x)` as `x * rsqrt_special(x)` (captured from its shader cache).

## Exhaustive results (hardware)

Correct rounding of sqrt depends only on the mantissa and the exponent's parity, and sqrt has no rounding ties. Every
f32 in [1, 4) therefore covers every case in the normal range. Each input ran on the GPU and was compared with an
integer-`isqrt` reference.

| Sequence (native fp32) | Ops | Every input in [1, 4) (16,777,216) |
|---|---|---|
| Mesa: `x * rsqrt_special(x)` | 2 | 5,430,945 misrounded (32%), within 2 ulp of correctly rounded |
| **one correction**: `y = rsqrt_special(x); s = x*y; r = fma(-s, s, x); s = fma(r, 0.5*y, s)` | **5** | **0 wrong** |
| coupled (Markstein-style: `s`, `h = y/2`, `e = fma(-s, h, 0.5)`, update both, final correction) | 8 | 0 wrong |

**Full range:** all 256 exponents × 65,536 mantissas, 16,777,216 positive inputs including subnormals.
- The one-correction sequence fails only at biased exponents 0-24, where subnormal or tiny inputs flush the remainder.
  It is exact at 25-254 on these samples.
- `sqrt_fast_ok` (1 integer compare: `0x18000000 ≤ bits < 0x7f800000`, so positive, exponent ≥ 48) keeps a margin.
  Everything else goes to the soft path.
- Soft `fsqrt32` (bit-by-bit restoring, 25 bits plus sticky, all four rounding modes, ≈ 470 ops) is **bit-exact on all
  16,777,216** inputs.
- The **hybrid** is **bit-exact on all 16,777,216** inputs.
- Negative inputs, ±0, inf and NaN were checked in the interpreter against the rational reference, but not on hardware.

## Cost (dependent chains `x = sqrt(x)`, 131,072 lanes, clock held)

| Mesa | 0.16 | 1 |

## Compiler implications (ZLUDA)

- `sqrt.rn.f32`: use the 5-op sequence wherever the input is known to be ≥ 2^-79 or the kernel is `.ftz`. Otherwise use
  the hybrid. Exact sqrt is nearly free compared with exact division or fma.
- `sqrt.approx.f32`: Mesa's 2-op form is within 2 ulp of correctly rounded. Compare that with the PTX bound before
  relying on it; the bound was not checked here.
- `sqrt.rz/.rm/.rp.f32`: the soft path.

## Reproduce

`python3 -B scripts/run_drm_fsqrt32.py offline|hardware --seq mesa|one|coupled|hybrid|soft [--data binades|sweep] [--chain K]`.
Offline: `make verify-fsqrt32` (8 checks against [results/2026-10-20-fsqrt32](../../results/2026-10-20-fsqrt32)). The
records hold per-run mismatch counts; the 16.7M-element output arrays stay in scratch and can be regenerated by rerunning.
